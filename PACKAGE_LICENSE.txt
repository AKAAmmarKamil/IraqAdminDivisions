2. EMBEDDED DATA
======================================================================

The administrative divisions, names and boundaries embedded in these packages
come from the sources below. Free and commercial use is allowed; keep the
attribution.

a) UN OCHA, Common Operational Dataset - Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB)
   Iraq, via the Humanitarian Data Exchange
   (https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-irq).
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO (CC BY-IGO 3.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/
   The data was modified (corrected, restructured for Halabja Governorate,
   merged with other names, simplified and re-exported). This is not an
   official UN or OCHA product.

b) Wikidata (https://www.wikidata.org): Kurdish labels and ISO 3166-2 codes.
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).

c) aryzonedev/iraq-cities (https://github.com/aryzonedev/iraq-cities):
   some Kurdish names.
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
